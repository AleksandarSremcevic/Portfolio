About Me

I was born in Serbia, in a small town called Topola. My family has always been at the heart of everything I do, and I helped run our family business 'Igraonica Game House Topola.' At 15 and a half, I moved to Canada with my family and found a new life in Calgary. As a creative person with a passion for technology, I fell in love with the world of UX design and interactive websites. Currently, I'm studying Interactive Design - User Experience Design Major at SAIT - Southern Alberta Institute Of Technology, aiming to build designs that are not only functional but also beautiful. When I'm not immersed in design, or coding different things, you can find me gaming, exploring new ideas, or spending time with my amazing girlfriend, Amanda, who motivates me every day. I’m always looking for ways to grow and improve, both in my personal and professional life.
